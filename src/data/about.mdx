I have a background spanning both **information science** and **physics**. 
After leaving [the Faculty of Environment and Information Studies at Keio University](https://www.keio.ac.jp/en/sfc/), I transferred into the third year of [the Department of Physics at Hokkaido University](https://phys2.sci.hokudai.ac.jp/en/), and later entered [the Graduate School of Science at Kyoto University](https://sci.kyoto-u.ac.jp/en), where I am currently pursuing my Ph.D. in the [Nonlinear Dynamics Lab](https://www.ton.scphys.kyoto-u.ac.jp/en/) under the supervision of Prof. [Andreas Dechant](https://sites.google.com/site/dechantan/).
<br />
My research focuses on **constrained optimization problems arising in nonequilibrium systems**, using tools from stochastic processes, information theory, and deep neural networks. Especially, I am interested in adaptive systems that acquire, process, and lose information through learning. **My main motivation is to understand these processes as nonequilibrium phenomena and to develop a physical description of how information is accumulated, retained, and dissipated during learning**.
